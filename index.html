Effortless capture

Press record. That's your part done.

SailTies tracks the passage while you sail it: distance, duration, speed and the conditions you had. Add crew, add photos as you go, and it's filed by the time you're tied up.

“It's become a routine to start SailTies as soon as I start rigging the boat.”

The record

The trips you'd want

to look at again.

Every voyage keeps its map, its photos, the anchorage you'd go back to and the people who were aboard. Replay the passage, share a postcard from the day, or scroll back through a whole season.
